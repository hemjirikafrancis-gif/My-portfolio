About Us

Caring for Your Health Since 2014

GreenCross Pharmacy was founded with a simple but powerful mission — to make quality healthcare accessible and affordable for every Nigerian family. We are a NAFDAC-registered pharmaceutical outlet with a passionate team of licensed pharmacists and healthcare professionals.

From our flagship location, we serve thousands of patients, families, clinics, and corporate clients every month. Whether you need prescription medicines, wellness products, or medical advice, we are here for you every step of the way.

  • NAFDAC-registered and fully licensed pharmacy
  • Over 2,000 genuine pharmaceutical products in stock
  • Licensed pharmacists available 7 days a week
  • Fast same-day delivery within the city
  • Trusted by hospitals, clinics, and households
