Our Story

Who We Are

GreenCross Pharmacy was established in 2014 with a clear vision — to provide accessible, affordable, and trustworthy healthcare to every Nigerian. What started as a small community pharmacy has grown into a respected healthcare institution serving thousands of patients, families, clinics, and organisations every month.

We are NAFDAC-registered and fully licensed by the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria. Our team of certified pharmacists and healthcare professionals brings decades of combined expertise to every patient interaction — ensuring you always receive the best pharmaceutical care possible.

  • NAFDAC-registered pharmaceutical outlet
  • Licensed by the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria
  • Over 2,000 genuine products always in stock
  • Serving individuals, hospitals, and corporations
  • Same-day delivery available within the city
